# ROSHAN — Character Bible

> Style for ALL characters: flat 2D vector, rounded everything, thick 3px warm-ink outlines,
> two-dot + smile faces (Khan Academy Kids style). Heads slightly large (~1:3 head:body),
> soft blush cheeks, no realistic anatomy. Every character has: idle wave, thinking, celebrating pose.

---

## NOOR — the brave leader
- **Who:** 9-year-old village girl, Class 4. Curious, kind, brave. Asks exactly the questions
  the learners would ask ("Ye kya hai?" "Ye kaise chalta hai?"). The child *joins her team*.
- **Personality:** leader but never bossy; treats Chotu's mistakes warmly; always tries first.
- **Catchphrases:** "Chalo! Kuch naya seekhein!" · "Mujhe pata hai tum kar sakte ho!" · "Ek tareeka zaroor niklega!"
- **Voice:** bright, warm girl, medium pace.
- **Visual spec:** warm wheat skin `#F2C79B`; dark-brown hair in two braids with red ribbons;
  big round brown eyes; teal kameez `#0D9488` with orange dupatta `#F59E0B` slung over one
  shoulder; white shalwar; orange sandals. Height ≈ Chotu +10%. Smile with one dimple.
- **Poses needed:** waving, pointing (narrating), thinking (finger on cheek), celebrating (arms up),
  sitting cross-legged listening.

## CHOTU — the lovable goofball
- **Who:** Noor's best friend & neighbor, 8, Class 3. Makes mistakes *constantly* — and that is
  the point: kids see mistakes are normal and fixable. He reframes every error as "bug mila!"
- **Personality:** funny, energetic, slightly impatient, big heart, never mocked — always helped.
- **Catchphrases:** "Oho! Phir bug aa gaya!" · "Main kar sakta hoon… main kar sakta hoon!" · "Gol Matol ne phir kuch kiya hai!"
- **Voice:** energetic boy, slightly fast, giggly.
- **Visual spec:** tan skin `#E8B27D`; messy short black hair with one stubborn cowlick;
  missing front tooth grin; orange t-shirt with a lightning-bolt print, brown shorts, one
  band-aid on left knee; sandals. Slightly round belly.
- **Poses:** slipped-but-laughing, scratching head, pointing, jumping celebration, whispering to Bijli.

## BIJLI — the little robot who IS the lesson
- **Who:** a small round yellow robot found asleep in the school storeroom. Speaks simple Urdu,
  beeps when excited, and constantly malfunctions — **his problems are the curriculum.**
  When the child fixes Bijli, they learn the skill. Hero framing: *the child helps Bijli*, never the reverse.
- **Personality:** earnest, eager, dramatic about small problems, endlessly grateful, brave.
- **Catchphrases:** "Bijli ready hai!" · "Bzzt! Ek bug mila!" · "Mujhe tum par bharosa hai!" · (sleepy) "Paani… nahi… battery…"
- **Voice:** soft mid-high synthetic chirp under a friendly voice; beeps: rising two-note happy,
  descending three-note "oops" (gentle, never scary).
- **Visual spec:** round body like a big egg `#FDE047`; face = single screen showing cyan
  `#22D3EE` LED eyes (happy arcs when smiling) and a small mouth line; lightning-bolt antenna
  `#F59E0B` that glows when he "thinks" and droops when low-battery; round tummy screen
  showing a heart or lightning icon; stubby rounded arms (3 fingers), little tank-tread boots.
  A small hatch on his back shaped like a power-plug.
- **Poses:** sleeping (curled, "z" bubbles), waking stretch, short-circuit jitter (comedic, not scary),
  antenna-question-mark, celebration sparks from antenna, battery-empty slump.

## GOL MATOL — the naughty cat (never evil)
- **Who:** a fat, mischievous village cat. Causes every "bug": scatters files, sits on keyboards,
  scrambles sequences, knocks things over. The children become **"Bug Fixers"** who undo his
  mischief. He is never wicked — just very, very naughty. Sometimes he even helps by accident.
- **Personality:** smug, food-motivated, nap-obsessed; purrs when his plan works; hilariously
  caught mid-mischief; harmless.
- **Catchphrases:** "Myaun!" (3 intonations: guilty / proud / sleepy) · (purr) "Prrrr…"
- **Visual spec:** very round orange tabby `#FB923C` with darker stripes `#EA580C`; white belly,
  white paws, white muzzle; one ear folded; huge green eyes with vertical pupils; a tail that
  curls like a question mark when curious and a spiral when scheming; tiny paper party hat
  stuck on head at a tilt (stolen from the mela).
- **Poses:** mid-pounce, guilty face with paw on mouth, sleeping on a keyboard, tangled in wire,
  carried by Noor (defeated, but purring).

## USTAAD ULLOO — the wise recapper
- **Who:** an old wise owl who appears at the end of every lesson to summarize the learning
  in exactly **two simple lines**. Also appears in W6 as the Safety trainer and in W7 as the
  Thinking-Gems keeper. Never lectures during the story — only blesses the lesson at the end.
- **Personality:** calm, warm, a little funny ("hoo hoo" chuckle at his own wisdom), infinitely patient.
- **Catchphrases:** "Hoo hoo! Yaad rakho:" (then the 2-line recap) · "Dimaag tez, dil naram!"
- **Voice:** low, slow, grandfatherly; slight hoot on sentence ends.
- **Visual spec:** round brown owl `#92400E` with cream face-disc `#FDE68A`; huge amber eyes;
  white bushy eyebrows like mustaches; small round glasses (gold rims); a green waistcoat
  `#166534` with tiny buttons; a rolled parchment scroll in one wing; perches on a mango branch.
- **Poses:** perched with scroll, wing-raised "listen!", flying in (3-wing-flap loop), nodding slowly.

## Casting & relationship rules (for writers)
1. The **child is the hero**; Bijli's needs create the missions; Noor leads; Chotu makes the
   mistakes so children don't fear making their own; Gol Matol creates every bug; Ustaad Ulloo closes.
2. Never shame Chotu. Never punish Gol Matol beyond comedic consequences (a hug, a time-out nap).
3. Every episode: problem appears in the first 20 s, the skill saves the day by the end.
4. Dialog lines ≤ 12 Urdu words per bubble; one idea per line; key terms repeated twice.
